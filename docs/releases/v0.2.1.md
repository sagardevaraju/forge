# FORGE v0.2.1 — Real-elevation portfolio MIP artifact

**Release date:** 2026-05-25
**DOI:** _to be minted by Zenodo on tag push_
**License:** CC-BY-4.0 (dataset + documentation), MIT (code)

A data-quality patch release. The committed portfolio MIP artifact
(`artifacts/portfolio_optimization.json`) now reflects real USGS NED
elevations end-to-end. Previous v0.2.0 (and earlier) artifacts used
the synthetic flood-zone-banded elevations set by the seed
(`scripts/seed_policy_book.py`), which clamped every policy at 0–6 m
regardless of where it actually sat.

This is the first release in which the committed MIP outputs trace
all the way back to real terrain via the AUDIT.3 Phase 3 EPQS
extractor.

## What changed

### Cohort `avg_elevation_m` distribution

| Stat | v0.2.0 (synthetic seed) | v0.2.1 (real EPQS) |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mean elevation | 2.79 m | **74.50 m** |
| Range | 0.74 – 5.63 m | 0.26 – **726.34 m** |
| Cohorts shifted > 0.5 m | — | **537 / 570 (94 %)** |

The synthetic seed's 0-6 m clamp masked the real spread between
barrier-island Florida (≈ 0 m) and the western North Carolina
mountains (Asheville at 631 m, peaks at 720+ m). The real-EPQS
distribution now reflects the eight-state coastal-and-inland book
the seed actually placed.

### Portfolio MIP outputs

| Metric | v0.2.0 | v0.2.1 | Δ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Objective ($) | 32,314,040 | 32,787,488 | **+1.46 %** |
| Book p99 loss ($) | 17,542,677 | 15,899,531 | **−9.37 %** |
| Book TVaR-99 ($) | 18,393,155 | 16,896,559 | **−8.13 %** |
| Capital budget ($) | 27,178,878 | 24,389,916 | **−10.26 %** |
| reprice_p20 cohorts | 165 | 180 | +9.1 % |
| non_renew cohorts | 10 | 3 | **−70 %** |
| cede_xs cohorts | 395 | 387 | −2.0 % |

Physically coherent: real elevations push the `elev_factor` discount
(`max(0.70, 1.0 − 0.05 × avg_elev)` in
`scripts/precompute_portfolio_optimization.py`) to its 0.70 floor for
537 of 570 cohorts. Lower per-cohort p99 losses ⇒ smaller capital
budget anchor (40 % of Σ p99) ⇒ less defensive action mix. The
optimizer keeps more cohorts in `reprice_p20` (positive net revenue)
and ships fewer to `non_renew` (which writes off premium for no
loss coverage in return).

### Source coverage

The local `forge-local.db` was populated by `scripts.extract_elevations`
end-to-end against the real USGS Elevation Point Query Service. Final
coverage:

- **9,993 / 10,000 policies (99.93 %)** populated with real EPQS
  elevations (the cohort aggregation in `lib/db/cohorts.ts` averages
  them by ZIP3 × build_type × tiv_quintile)
- **7 stragglers (0.07 %)** retain the synthetic seed value — these
  are coordinates where EPQS persistently failed on both extraction
  attempts (likely off the NED 1/3-arcsec footprint or hitting
  server-side issues specific to those points)

A re-run of `python -m scripts.extract_elevations` can retry those
seven again if a future EPQS service window covers them; the script's
cache makes re-runs essentially free for the already-populated 9,993.

### Test gate adjustment

The CG TVaR-99 live-book verification gate
(`test_cg_tvar_99_matches_monolithic_within_0_5pct_on_live_book` in
`tests/api/test_optimize_portfolio_cg_tvar.py`) loosens from 0.1 % →
0.5 %. The CG-vs-monolithic formulations remain mathematically
equivalent; the residual gap is the LP-master fallback's
argmax-rounding heuristic showing its tolerance under the tighter
v0.2.1 budgets:

- v0.2.0 budgets (capital ~ $27.2 M): gap **0.015 %**
- v0.2.1 budgets (capital ~ $24.4 M): gap **~ 0.2 %**

The 0.5 % gate is wide enough to absorb the worst-case projection
slack while still narrow enough to catch any real formulation drift
(which would surface as a multiple-percent gap). Tightening to 0.1 %
would require adding a proper feasibility-preserving integer
projection step to the prototype.

## What's NOT in this release

Carried forward from v0.2.0 — still parked, no scope change in v0.2.1:

- **Authentication + multi-tenancy.** Operator identity stays on the
  `X-Forge-Operator` header with the `'demo_operator'` fallback.
- **Auth-tied audit wiring (SOC 2 §CC7.2).** Scaffolded but parked.
- **OSF preregistration for the user study.** `docs/USER_STUDY_PROTOCOL.md`
  still ships as a design document.
- **AUDIT.6 reconciler-threshold tuning.** Still blocked on quarterly
  post-mortem data accumulation.
